## Mistake taxonomy

- Misread requirement.
- Missed edge case.
- Wrong data structure.
- Off-by-one.
- Complexity too high.
- Could not explain approach clearly.
- Debugging took too long.
- Did not test with small examples.

## Review rules

- Review wrong or slow problems after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days.
- Rewrite the approach in plain English before re-coding.
- Record the invariant or core trick.
- Re-solve without looking at the previous solution.
