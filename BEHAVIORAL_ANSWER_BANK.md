## Story prompts

### Technical challenge

- What was hard?
- What constraints made it hard?
- What options did you consider?
- What did you choose and why?
- What was the measurable result?

### Production issue

- What broke?
- How did users or systems notice?
- How did you debug?
- How did you mitigate?
- What permanent fix or prevention did you add?

### Conflict

- What was the disagreement?
- What was your position?
- How did you listen and adapt?
- What decision was made?
- What did you learn?

### Ownership

- What problem did you take responsibility for?
- What did you do without being asked?
- How did you communicate progress?
- What changed because of your ownership?
